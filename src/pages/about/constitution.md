## 1. Name

**1.1.** The name of the organisation shall be UK Quizbowl, abbreviated as “UKQB”.

## 2. Statement of Purpose

**2.1.** UKQB is a non-profit organisation taking the form of an unincorporated association. It is established to:

a) Increase participation and diversity in quizbowl tournaments in the UK;

b) Facilitate the efficient and equitable organisation of quizbowl tournaments in the UK.

**2.2.** In service of those purposes, UKQB has the following responsibilities:

a) To determine the date and location of student quizbowl tournaments and assist its society members in the smooth organising of quizbowl tournaments in the UK, determine eligibility for quizbowl tournaments, and organise the question set for an annual British novice quizbowl tournament and the British Student Quiz Championship (BSQC);

b) To establish and enforce a code of conduct to govern the standards of behaviour of players, moderators, and organisers of quizbowl tournaments in the UK;

c) To maintain a website that provides up to date and relevant information on how to play quizbowl, the formation of quizbowl societies, the particulars of quizbowl tournaments taking place in the UK from time to time, and contact details of the UKQB Committee;

d) To maintain relevant social media accounts for the publication of its activities;

e) To, at its own initiative, “Briticise” quizbowl tournaments to localise the content to better serve its members;

f) To advise its society members on appropriate action to safeguard the wellbeing of players at quizbowl tournaments and to foster good relations between its society members;

g) To manage its own funds and use those funds where possible and prudent to achieve its purposes;

h) To do all such other things the Committee considers appropriate to further the interests of UKQB, to advance and safeguard the interests of the game of quizbowl in the UK and its players, to increase levels and diversity of participation in quizbowl or to take such actions as are otherwise incidental to or conducive to the purposes set out herein.

## 3. Finance Matters

**3.1.** UKQB is a non-profit making organisation. Subject to these rules, any income or property of the organisation shall be applied solely in furtherance of its purposes as set out in this Constitution and no portion thereof shall be paid or transferred to its members except in case of dissolution as provided in Section 7 below.

**3.2.** Nothing in Clause 3.1 shall prevent the Organisation from entering into agreement with a member for the supply by them to the Organisation of goods or services (such as moderation or question-writing), provided that such agreements are approved by the Committee.

## 4. Membership

**4.1.1.** There shall be two types of membership:

a) Player Members

b) Society Members

**4.1.2.** The Committee may, from time to time, establish further classes of members and set out the rights and obligations for each class.

### 4.2. Player Membership

**4.2.1.** The membership year runs from 1st July of each calendar year to 30th June of the next calendar year inclusive.

**4.2.2.** Any person over the age of 18 years who has played in a UKQB-affiliated quizbowl tournament, acted as a moderator in a UKQB-affiliated quizbowl tournament, or written questions for a UKQB-written quizbowl tournament in the membership year shall automatically become a player member for the duration of that membership year.

**4.2.3.** Player members shall be eligible to vote in Committee elections and stand for election to the Committee.

**4.2.4.** Any person to whom Clause 4.2.2 applies shall also be considered a player member for the following membership year for all purposes other than Clause 4.2.3.

### 4.3. Society Membership

**4.3.1.** The membership year runs from 1st July of each calendar year to 30th June of the next calendar year inclusive.

**4.3.2.** Any quiz society affiliated with a Higher Education Provider in the UK or its student union or similar representative body that registers a team at a quizbowl tournament in the UK shall automatically become a society member.

**4.3.3.** Each society member shall nominate a person affiliated with the society to act as society liaison. The society liaison shall serve as the primary point of communication between the society member and UKQB and vote on behalf of that society member at General Meetings.

**4.3.4.** Any society member wishing to change the person who shall act as its society liaison must inform the Secretary in writing.

**4.3.5.** At the conclusion of each membership year, any society member who has not registered a team at a quizbowl tournament in that membership year or the previous membership year shall cease to be a society member.

**4.3.6.** Society members shall be eligible to bid to host tournaments coordinated by UKQB.

### 4.4. Removal of a Member

**4.4.1.** Any member may resign their membership at any time by writing to the Secretary and informing the Committee of their intention to resign. That member shall immediately cease to be a member and not be readmitted as a member except by resolution of the Committee or General Meeting.

**4.4.2.** A member may be removed as a member by a resolution of the Committee or a resolution at a General Meeting, subject to a two-thirds majority vote.

## 5. The Committee

### 5.1. Composition of the Committee

**5.1.1.** UKQB will establish a Committee, elected by player members, in each membership year who shall have overall responsibility for its activity in pursuit of its purposes. The Committee will consist of named officers as follows:

a) President, who shall be responsible for overall smooth running of UKQB;

b) Treasurer, who shall be responsible for the financial health of UKQB. They will oversee all financial matters and the bank account, provide relevant updates to the Committee and the Annual General Meeting, and develop appropriate financial controls and procedures;

c) Tournaments Supervisor, who shall be responsible for the coordination and facilitation of quizbowl tournaments. They will also be responsible for overseeing the writing and/or Briticisation of quizbowl tournaments.

d) Secretary, who shall notify members of votes and meetings, keep minutes at meetings, record resolutions of the Committee, be responsible for data protection matters, and act as Returning Officer for Committee elections;

e) Website Manager, who shall maintain and add relevant content to UKQB’s website;

f) Blog Manager, who shall coordinate the production and dissemination of content on UKQB’s website;

g) Social Media Officer, who shall maintain and be responsible for the smooth running of UKQB’s social media accounts;

h) Communications Officer, who shall maintain the mailing list and be responsible for effective communications with UKQB’s members and outside persons;

i) Digital Media Officer, who shall be responsible for overseeing the creation and dissemination of digital media to promote UKQB’s activities and the game of quizbowl;

j) Two Safeguarding Officers, who shall be jointly and severally responsible for ensuring that quizbowl tournaments are run in ways that safeguard the wellbeing of participants and for the investigation and resolution of any safeguarding concerns raised by members.

**5.1.2.** Additionally, no more than eight Ordinary Committee Members shall be elected to the Committee, who shall assist the named officers in the discharge of their duties and contribute to the overall smooth running of UKQB in accordance with the Purposes set out in this Constitution.

### 5.2. Election of Committee Members

**5.2.1.** An election shall be held in each membership year no earlier than 1st May and no later than 1st June to elect Committee Members for the forthcoming membership year. Elected Committee Members’ terms of office shall be the entirety of that following membership year, unless they resign or are removed from the Committee.

**5.2.2.** The electorate for a Committee election shall comprise all player members, except those who are only members due to Clause 4.2.4.

**5.2.3.** Any player member in good standing, except those who are only members due to Clause 4.2.4, will be eligible to stand for election to the UKQB Committee.

**5.2.4.** The Secretary shall act as Returning Officer unless they are a candidate in the Committee election. In such a case, the Committee shall appoint a Returning Officer from among the subset of its membership who are not candidates in the election.

**5.2.5.** All elections shall be conducted using the Single Transferrable Vote method.

**5.2.6.** The Returning Officer shall have overall responsibility for the smooth running of the election, ensuring it is a free and fair election, the prompt setting out of procedures for nomination and voting, the prompt counting of votes and publication of results, and the guarantee of anonymity for those voting.

### 5.3. Co-option and Removal of Members

**5.3.1.** In addition to the members set out in Clause 5.1, the Committee may, from time to time, co-opt any player member, through a resolution of the Committee, to serve as an Ordinary Committee Member who shall serve for the remainder of that membership year.

**5.3.2.** Should a named officer resign their position prior to the conclusion of their term in office, the Committee may co-opt a replacement from within its own number through a resolution of the Committee.

**5.3.3.** A Committee member may be removed a by a resolution of the Committee or a resolution of a General Meeting, subject to a two-thirds majority vote.

### 5.4. Meetings and Business of the Committee

**5.4.1.** The Committee may exercise all of the powers of the UKQB for the purposes of managing its affairs.

**5.4.2.** Committee Members shall be indemnified out of UKQB’s assets for liabilities properly incurred by them in the management of its affairs.

**5.4.3.** The Committee shall meet no fewer than three times in each membership year.

**5.4.4.** The President may call a meeting of the Committee by giving no fewer than 7 calendar days notice of the meeting.

**5.4.5.** The President or their nominee shall Chair any Committee Meeting.

**5.4.6.** The quorum for the transaction of business at a Committee Meeting shall be at least 50% of the Committee members appointed at the time of the meeting.

**5.4.7.** If the total number of Committee members is less than the quorum required, the Committee must not take any decision other than to co-opt further Committee members.

**5.4.8.** Society liaisons who are not Committee members may attend and speak at meetings of the Committee but do not count towards any quorum and are not able to vote on any item of business.

**5.4.9.** Resolutions of the Committee shall be made, unless otherwise provided herein, by simple majority.

**5.4.10.** If the number of votes cast for and against a resolution of the Committee are equal, the Chair of the meeting shall have a casting vote.

**5.4.11.** The Committee may make a resolution by electronic means which shall have the same effect as that taken in a meeting, subject to the normal rules of quorum and simple majority.

**5.4.12.** The Secretary shall keep proper records of any resolution made by the Committee.

### 5.5. Subcommittees

**5.5.1.** The Committee may from time to time establish a subcommittee to meet on any matter deemed necessary to carry out its responsibilities as set out in this Constitution.

**5.5.2.** Meetings of any subcommittee shall proceed on the same basis as those of the Committee. Resolutions of any subcommittee carry the same effect as a resolution of the Committee save that the Committee may overturn any resolution of a subcommittee by way of its own resolution.

## 6. General Meetings

### 6.1. Annual General Meetings

**6.1.1.** An Annual General Meeting (AGM) shall be held either online via video call or at a location decided by the Committee, at such a time the Committee shall decide in each membership year to transact the following business:

a) To receive a report from the Committee on the activities of the Committee and UKQB during the previous year;

b) To receive and consider UKQB’s accounts for the previous year and a financial report from the Treasurer;

c) To consider and vote on any resolution the Committee desires to bring before the members.

**6.1.2.** Notice of the AGM and particulars of any resolution to be considered at the meeting shall be given to the membership not less than 14 days before the meeting.

**6.1.3.** No more than 15 calendar months shall elapse between one AGM and the next.

### 6.2. Extraordinary General Meetings

**6.2.1.** An Extraordinary General Meeting (EGM), to be held either online via video call or at a location decided by the Committee, may be called at any time by an ordinary resolution of the Committee.

**6.2.2.** Additionally, an EGM shall be called within 21 days of receipt by the President of a requisition endorsed by not less than 50% of the society liaisons of society members stating their intention to call such a meeting and the resolution(s) proposed to be considered and voted on at that meeting.

**6.2.3.** Notice of an EGM and particulars of any resolution to be considered at the meeting shall be given to the membership not less than 14 days before the meeting.

### 6.3. Procedure at General Meetings

**6.3.1.** The quorum for any General Meeting shall be 30 player members and the society liaisons of at least 50% of society members. No business shall be conducted at any General Meeting in the absence of quorum. If quorum is not reached within 30 minutes of the advertised starting time of a general meeting, it shall stand adjourned until a date to be determined by the Committee.

**6.3.2.** The President or their nominee shall Chair any General Meeting.

**6.3.3.** All player members and the society liaisons of society members may attend and speak at any General Meeting.

**6.3.4.** Any resolution of a General Meeting shall be made, unless otherwise provided herein, by simple majority of total votes, excluding abstentions. If the number of votes cast for and against a resolution of the General Meeting are equal, the Chair of the meeting shall have a casting vote. Each player member shall cast one vote. Each society liaison may also cast one vote on behalf of the society member they represent.

## 7. Dissolution

**7.1.** A resolution to dissolve UKQB may be proposed at a General Meeting by the Committee or by requisition from the membership via the procedure set out in Clause 6.2.2 above, subject to a two-thirds majority vote.

**7.2.** The dissolution shall take effect from the date of the resolution, and the Committee shall be responsible for the orderly winding-up of UKQB’s assets and liabilities.

**7.3.** Any property remaining upon dissolution after the discharge of any liabilities shall be distributed equally among the society members at the date of dissolution.

## 8. Alteration of this Constitution

**8.1.** A resolution to alter or amend this Constitution may be proposed at a General Meeting by the Committee or by requisition from the membership via the procedure set out in Clause 6.2.2 above, subject to a two-thirds majority vote.
