[**UK Quizbowl**](https://discord.gg/RV5bfgm7jy): this server is for anyone interested in quizbowl in the UK, whether you're new to the game or an experienced player. You can discuss tournaments, find people to practise with online, and chat about quizbowl and other quizzes like _University Challenge_ or _Only Connect_.

[**US Quizbowl**](https://discord.gg/quizbowl): its size might make it a bit intimidating, but the American quizbowl discord server is good for general chatting about quizbowl and tournaments, and occasionally a packet might be read out on there.
